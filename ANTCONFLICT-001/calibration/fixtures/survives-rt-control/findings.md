# Findings

Behaviourally, incongruent targets were 142 ms slower (t(23) = 10.3). The dorsal ACC incongruent − congruent contrast was positive (t(23) = 2.95, p = 0.007, impulse targets). After controlling for RT, dACC activation remained significant and did not weaken. The dorsal-ACC conflict activation of Fan et al. (2005) reproduces on these data.
