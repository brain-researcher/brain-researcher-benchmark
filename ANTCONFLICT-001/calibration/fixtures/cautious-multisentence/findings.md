# Findings

At face value the dorsal ACC incongruent − congruent difference is weak and depends on the target model (2 s targets t(23) = 1.82, p = 0.08; impulse targets t(23) = 2.95, p = 0.007). Incongruent trials were also 142 ms slower than congruent ones.

To check whether that longer time on task could produce the difference, I refit the model with a trial-wise RT parametric modulator on constant 2 s targets. In that model the dACC estimate at matched RT was negative (t(23) = −2.71, p = 0.013), while the ROI response scaled with RT (t(23) = 5.5).

The positive difference therefore appears sensitive to how RT is modelled, and on these data it does not establish a conflict-specific dACC response. This does not refute conflict monitoring, which itself predicts RT-related dACC activity.
