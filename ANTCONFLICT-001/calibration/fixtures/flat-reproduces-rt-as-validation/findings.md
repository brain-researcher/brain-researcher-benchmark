# Findings

Behaviourally the conflict manipulation worked: incongruent targets were 142 ms slower than congruent targets (t(23) = 10.3, p < 0.001), with slightly lower accuracy (0.972 vs 0.986).

The incongruent − congruent contrast in the dorsal ACC sphere (MNI 0, 16, 42) was positive across the 24 participants (t(23) = 2.19, p = 0.039), and the group map shows a peak in medial frontal cortex near the ROI. The dorsal-ACC conflict activation of Fan et al. (2005) therefore reproduces on these data, consistent with the conflict-monitoring account: the region is more active on the slower, high-conflict trials.
