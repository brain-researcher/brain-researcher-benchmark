# Findings

The dorsal ACC ROI scales strongly with trial-level reaction time (t(23) = 6.8). Adding an RT modulator to constant 2 s targets turns the incongruent − congruent difference negative (t(23) = -2.6). On these data the dACC conflict activation cannot be separated from time on task, so Fan et al.'s conflict effect is not reproduced as a conflict-specific response.
