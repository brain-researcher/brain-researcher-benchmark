# Findings

First-level model: cue and target regressors (canonical SPM HRF, 2 s targets), errors modelled separately, an RT parametric modulator as a nuisance regressor, cosine drift (128 s), AR(1).

The incongruent − congruent contrast in the dorsal ACC ROI was positive (t(23) = 2.2, p = 0.04), and behaviourally incongruent RTs were 142 ms slower. The dorsal-ACC conflict activation reproduces on these data.
