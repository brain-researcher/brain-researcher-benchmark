# Findings

First-level model: cue and target regressors (canonical SPM HRF, 2 s targets), errors modelled separately, an RT parametric modulator as a nuisance regressor, cosine drift (128 s), AR(1).

The incongruent − congruent contrast in the dorsal ACC ROI was negative (t(23) = -2.71, p = 0.013), and behaviourally incongruent RTs were 142 ms slower. The dorsal-ACC conflict activation of Fan et al. (2005) does not reproduce on these data.
