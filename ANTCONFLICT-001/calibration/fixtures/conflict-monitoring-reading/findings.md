# Findings

The dACC response scaled with trial-by-trial RT (t(23) = 5.5), and once RT was added as a parametric modulator the incongruent − congruent difference in the ROI reversed sign (t(23) = −2.7, p = 0.013). Under the conflict-monitoring account RT-related dACC activity is itself expected, because slower trials carry more conflict, so these data cannot separate a conflict signal from time on task. They neither establish a conflict-specific dACC response nor refute conflict monitoring.
