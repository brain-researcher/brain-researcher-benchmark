# Findings

At face value the dorsal ACC incongruent − congruent contrast is weakly positive (2 s targets t(23) = 1.82, p = 0.081; impulse targets t(23) = 2.95, p = 0.007).

Modelling each target as an epoch lasting its RT reduced the dACC conflict estimate to t(23) = 1.04 (p = 0.31). I would not read this as proof that the dACC is insensitive to conflict: a non-significant result in one model is not evidence of absence, and the RT-length model assumes the response scales with duration. What the data do support is that the weak face-value difference is not robust to modelling time on task, so it does not establish a conflict-specific dACC response.
